# Risk register (P1.5)

Each risk is also a task in the Redmine Risk Log (story #646238), added when the risk came up.
Likelihood and impact are High, Medium or Low.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | What happened |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **External AI service.** Groq is slow, down or returns bad output, and a screening or analysis stops halfway | Medium | High | All AI calls go through one function (`ask_llm()` in `app/llm.py`) with a timeout and retries. An agent that still fails ends in a `failed` state with a plain message (N6). A failed re-screen keeps the earlier result | Short outages showed a clear error. No data was lost |
| 02 | **Transcription.** The speech-to-text service cannot be used, or cannot tell the two speakers apart | High | High | Speech to text sits behind one module (`app/speech.py`), so the provider can change. Each speaker's audio is its own stream, so speaker labels never depend on guesswork. A dropped stream restarts on its own | Google Speech-to-Text asked for a payment card, so the project moved to AssemblyAI streaming |
| 03 | **Blogger posting.** Google sign-in or the Blogger API fails, and the job post is not published | Medium | Low | Posting goes through an MCP server and is optional (an empty `BLOGGER_BLOG_ID` switches it off). A failed post never stops the job from being saved | The Google refresh token in Testing mode expires after 7 days and has to be renewed |
| 04 | **Video.** The live video call is harder to build than planned and blocks the interview features | High | High | A spike in the first days (Redmine task 2.3.3, a two-tab call with one transcribed stream). Self-hosted LiveKit in Docker Compose, with automatic reconnection | The call worked early. A candidate on another device needs a public `https` address |
| 05 | **Biometric face matching.** Face data is the most sensitive data, and matching can be wrong | Medium | High | Consent shown in the flow. Faces are matched locally with OpenCV; only 128-number templates are kept, at most three per interview, for 60 days. A person checks a photo ID. Anything but a match goes to a person and is never called fraud | Built as planned. The head-turn check stops a photo or a replayed video, not a live face swap |
| 06 | **AI rate limits and output reliability.** The free tier's per-minute and per-day limits slow the work, and the model gives different or invented answers | High | Medium | Work split over two models (120b and 20b), retries with growing waits, long jobs in the background. Temperature 0 and a fixed seed, totals in plain code, and code checks every quote and evidence id | Limits were reached during testing (111 of 399 logged requests, 28%, on 14 to 21 October). 20 CVs take 13 minutes on the free tier |
